What is Phishing?

Phishing is a type of cyber attack where attackers impersonate trusted individuals or organizations to trick users into revealing sensitive information, clicking malicious links, or downloading harmful files.

🎣
📧

Suspicious Email Content

Phishing emails often contain urgent messages, unexpected requests, fake offers, or unusual language.

🔗

Malicious URLs

Attackers may use suspicious links that redirect users to fake login pages or malicious websites.

⚠️

Social Engineering

Phishing attacks use psychological techniques such as urgency, fear, rewards, and authority.
